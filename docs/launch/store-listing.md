## Full description

Pitcall is for small barbecue restaurants and food trucks that cook overnight for the next day and sell until the meat runs
out.

Every evening it answers one question: how many of each meat should go on the smoker tonight so you sell out near closing,
without running out early or throwing away a pile of leftovers?

HOW IT WORKS
- Type your own number first, then see Pitcall's number and a range of counts that are almost as good, so you can round to
  what fits your smoker.
- Get put-on times worked backward from opening, with the hold time shown.
- Tap one big button when a meat sells out. Pitcall treats a sell-out as a floor on demand, not the whole story, and estimates
  what you could have sold.
- Close out in about 30 seconds: leftover pounds, what they became, catering pounds.
- See each week how Pitcall's numbers compared with your own.

BUILT FOR A SMOKEHOUSE
- Big buttons, readable in a dark kitchen, works with gloves.
- Works with no signal. Everything stays on your phone; no account needed.
- One-tap backup file you save to iCloud or Google Drive.

Pitcall gets better as it learns your joint. Expect its first weeks to lean on the starting guesses you give it.

Pitcall is a planning aid. It does not replace your judgment or your local food-safety rules.
