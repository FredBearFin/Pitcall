Pitcall is a phone app that helps a barbecue restaurant or food truck decide how much meat to cook and when to put it on.
This policy explains what happens to your information.

## The short version

Everything you type into Pitcall stays on your phone. We do not run a server that receives it, there are no accounts, and
the app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

## What stays on your phone

Pitcall stores the following on your device only: your menu (meat names, prices, weights, yields), your plans, your
sell-out and "half gone" times, leftover and catering pounds, your logged cook times, and your settings. We cannot see this
information and we do not receive a copy.

Deleting the app deletes this data from the phone. Backup files you made yourself (see below) are not deleted by
uninstalling and remain yours to manage.

## Backups

Pitcall can export your data as a backup file and import one later. You choose where the file goes (for example iCloud
Drive, Google Drive, email or a computer). That storage is governed by that provider's terms, not ours. If you send us a
backup file to get help, we use it only to help you and we delete it when we are done.

## Notifications

Put-on reminders are scheduled by your phone itself ("local notifications"). We do not operate a push-notification server
and receive nothing about your alarms.

## Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your card number or Apple or Google
payment details. We receive only whether a subscription is active.

## What we do not do

We do not sell or rent your information, show ads, build advertising profiles, or track you across other apps or websites.

## Children

Pitcall is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Security

Your data is held in the app's private storage on your phone and is protected by your phone's own security (lock screen,
device encryption). Protect your phone and your backup files the way you would protect your business records.

## Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
